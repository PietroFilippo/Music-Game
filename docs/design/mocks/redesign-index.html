The problem, in one pictureToday at 1920 × 1032 the quiz is a 760 px column: a 560 px neck, 500 px of empty screen below it, and feedback that slides over the question. The three directions below all start from this screenshot.

Audit summaryConfirmed against the screenshots and the code. The full audit with evidence is in docs/design/REDESIGN.md.

Space

Page column capped at 760 px (1160 on Home); fretboard capped at 560 px (Fretboard.tsx WIDTH); VexFlow staves at 200–280 px. At 1920 the quiz uses 29 % of the width and the lesson leaves 700 px between the visual and Previous/Next.

Home

25 topic cards with 50 buttons; the 1920 page is 2697 px tall, the phone page 4000+. The Continue card is 320 px wide and sticky over empty space. Each module’s most prominent control is “Hide topics”.

Feedback covers the question

The fixed sheet (max-height 78vh) hides the board the learner just tapped; the solution is redrawn smaller inside the sheet. On phones the sheet covers the whole neck.

Target string

“Find D on string 6” draws no highlight: naturals.ts passes no highlightStrings, although Fretboard.tsx supports it and intervals.ts uses it. One-line fix, independent of the redesign.

Hierarchy and reading

Everything centered; quiz prompt 19 px; Stats numbers 13 px; chips 13 px; fret numbers 11 px inside a scaled-down SVG; uppercase tracked labels in seven places.

Navigation

Results offer Play again and Back, not the lesson. Stats and Tuner are reachable only from Home. No keyboard shortcut answers a question or continues.

Consistency

Card borders (#333 on #242424) are 1.2 : 1, invisible; secondary buttons and answer buttons have 1.4 : 1 borders. Amber means both “root” and “low score”. Fret cells on desktop are 38 × 34 px, under the 44 px target.

Stats and Tuner

Stats lists 25 rows with “—” for 22 of them and a disabled trash icon on each; no column headers. The Tuner is a 560 px column whose panel shows a sentence until the microphone is on; the meter is 18 px with no scale.

Three directionsEach board shows every screen at 1920 × 1032, 1366 × 768, 1100 × 720 and 390 × 844, with one Portuguese check.

PaletteSame identity: dark, mint accent, paper for notation. The greys get a trace of green so the mint belongs to them, borders become visible where they matter, and amber is named for what it does.

Control boundaries are the one pair under 3 : 1 in both palettes. The proposal raises them from 1.4 : 1 to 2.7–3.0 : 1 and never relies on the border alone: answer buttons and secondary buttons are also filled (--elevated) and carry text; the focus ring is a separate 12 : 1 colour. Amber marks roots and low scores but never alone: roots carry an “R”, low scores a “low” label, the tuner an arrow and words.

Type and spacingTwo families, two jobs. Segoe UI Variable (Text for body, Display for headings) is the system face on Windows 11 and falls back to system-ui elsewhere, so nothing is downloaded. Bahnschrift, also shipped with Windows, is the readout face: scores, round counts, timer, cents, fret numbers in tables. It is the typographic cousin of a tuner display and an amp faceplate; on other platforms it falls back to the display face with tabular numerals.
