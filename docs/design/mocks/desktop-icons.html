App icon and tray icon: three concepts

All three keep the app’s dark tile (--bg-card #242424 with a --border-strong rim) or its mint accent (--accent #6ee7b7), rounded like a Windows 11 app tile. Each concept has one master SVG for 256–32 px, one small master for 24 and 16 px (the installer, Start list, taskbar and Alt-Tab use these), and tray masters that drop the tile and follow the taskbar theme.

Marker A note on a string, or a fret marker under a fret wire: the two things the app teaches, in one shape. The white string line carries the icon at 16 px; the mint dot is the only colour.

Grid A mint tile with a three-string, two-fret grid and one finger. The brightest of the three in a taskbar full of blue and white icons; reads as “guitar” before “music”.

Monogram A lowercase m drawn as three strokes with two arches, echoing the 800-weight wordmark and, sideways, three strings. Mint on the dark tile.

App icon (Tauri icons/)

  • icon.ico with 256, 48, 32, 24, 16 (24 and 16 from the small master)
  • icon.png 512 for the bundler; 128x128.png, 128x128@2x.png, 32x32.png
  • Square Store logos only if an MSIX is ever built
  • Render from the SVG tile; do not add a drop shadow, Windows 11 does not

Tray icon

  • tray-dark-16/20/24/32.png (white glyph) and tray-light-… (dark glyph); pick by reading SystemUsesLightTheme and swap with TrayIcon.set_icon
  • Padding: 1 px transparent all round at 16, 2 px at 32; Windows adds its own spacing
  • Keep the mint dot in both themes; it is the only brand colour in the tray

Legibility checks before export

  • Place the 16 px icon on #202020 and #f3f3f3 next to Explorer and Edge: the silhouette must differ from both
  • At 125% and 150% scaling Windows uses 20 and 24 px: check those, not just 16
  • The tray glyph must not look like a notification dot or a volume icon
