B — “Map”: a progress dashboard you navigate

The course is shown as a map: a sidebar (desktop) or module cards (mobile) with progress rings, and each module unfolds into a path of topic nodes with best scores. Quizzes use round dots and a card-attached timer; feedback replaces the answer bank in place; results compare this run with best and average and list every round. Stats becomes a real dashboard.

  • Rings and nodes show progress
  • Sidebar on desktop, cards on mobile
  • Feedback in place, fixed bottom bar
  • Scrolling neck with zone chips, 44 px cells
  • Round-by-round results

Surfaces

bg #1a1a1a · sidebar #202020 · card #242424 · “up next” tint #1f2b27 with #2f5c4c edge. Radius 10 (cards, buttons), 999 (chips, dots).

Progress language

Rings = best score (module ring = share of topics done). Dots = rounds in a quiz. Dashed = planned. Every ring also prints its number; dots carry ✓/✗ icons, never colour alone.

Targets and type

Buttons 48 px (44 px small/icon), answers 56 px, fretboard cells 44 × 40 px (scrolling neck). Type: 15 body, 18 prompt, 18–24 titles, 26 tiles. Focus: 2 px #6ee7b7 outline, 3 px offset.

Notation

Light paper (#fafafa) panels with black ink, as today. Denser than A: the dashboard shows more numbers per screen, so spacing is 12–14 px rather than 16.
