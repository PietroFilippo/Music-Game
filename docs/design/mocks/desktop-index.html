Desktop window chrome: three directions

musicgame is being wrapped as a frameless Tauri v2 window on Windows 11, so the app draws Minimize, Maximize and Close itself. These boards show three ways to merge those controls with the app’s existing top bar, on every screen, in both languages, at 1100 and 420 px wide, plus the app and tray icons, the tray menu and the Desktop section of Settings. The decision record and the full spec of the recommended direction are in docs/design/DESKTOP.md.

Shared what every direction has in common

Window

  • decorations: false, shadow: true: Windows 11 still rounds the corners (8 px) and draws the shadow
  • Default 1100 × 720, minimum 400 × 560, centred; the title is still set for the taskbar and Alt-Tab
  • The outer 5 px are Tauri’s borderless resize inset; it disappears when maximized
  • Double-click on any drag region toggles maximize (Tauri does this for data-tauri-drag-region)

Controls

  • Three buttons: Minimize, Maximize / Restore, Close, with aria-label and a native tooltip in the app language
  • Close hover is Windows red #C42B1C with a white glyph; everything else uses app tokens
  • The cluster has z-index 100, above the feedback sheet (10) and the settings backdrop (20)
  • Last in the tab order; focus ring drawn inside the hit area
  • Close hides to the tray; Quit lives in the tray menu

App bar

  • The page column stays 760 px (1160 on Home), so on windows wider than about 1060 px the controls sit in the margin and the bar is untouched
  • When the column would reach them, the bar’s padding-right grows by the cluster width: max(10px, 148px − (100cqw − 100%) / 2)
  • Under 480 px the wordmark drops from 22 to 20 px
