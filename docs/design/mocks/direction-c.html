musicgame · design direction C

C — “Plain”: one column, big targets, nothing nested

Everything is a single column of large text and full-width controls: section headers instead of cards, 64 px rows, 56 px buttons, a bottom tab bar. The fretboard question is answered with a grid of fret buttons under a context diagram. Notation is inverted to match the dark UI. It is the least work to build and the easiest to use one-handed.

  • Single column, 18 px body
  • No nested cards
  • Fret-button grid, 48×56 px
  • Verdict banner + sticky Continue
  • Inverted notation

Surfaces

bg #1a1a1a everywhere · #242424 only for answer buttons and notation panels · dividers #2e2e2e, section rules #333. Radius 10. No nested cards.

Text and accent

Body 18 px, prompts 22 px, titles 26–28 px, score 76 px. Mint only on the primary action, progress lines and ✓; red only on ✗. Amber flags a best score under 70% next to the number.

Targets

Buttons 56 px (big primary 68 px), answers 64 px, fret buttons 48 × 56 px, rows 64 px, links 48 px, icon buttons 44 px. Focus: 2 px #6ee7b7 outline, 3 px offset.

Notation (inverted)

VexFlow output on a #242424 panel with filter: invert(.92) so ink is #ececec on dark (contrast 12:1). It keeps one visual system, at the cost of looking less like printed music; falling back to paper panels is a one-line change.
