C — “Band”: the app bar becomes the window’s title band

The top bar leaves the 760 px page column and spans the whole window as a 48 px band in a slightly lifted surface (#202020, between --bg and --bg-card). Back, title, the app’s icon buttons and the three window controls all live in that band, edge to edge, like a real title bar that happens to hold the app’s navigation. Content stays centred beneath it.

  • One full-width band, 48 px
  • Lifted surface, hairline below
  • Back and title at the window’s left edge
  • Controls 46 × 48, band-height

Trade-offs

  • + Reads most like a desktop application; the band gives the frameless window a visible “handle”
  • + Largest drag area on wide windows
  • − Back and the quiz title sit 170 px left of the content column on an 1100 px window
  • − Changes the shared top bar for the web build too, or forks it per platform
  • − Sheet backdrop and focus trap need band-specific rules (inert app buttons while a sheet is open)
