A — “Session”: one next step, one screen at a time

The app becomes a practice-session flow. Home opens on Continue (the next recommended topic) and keeps modules as a compact progress list. Inside a quiz, the header carries the round strip, score pills and countdown ring; feedback arrives as a bottom sheet over the question so Continue never moves; settings are a sheet you can open mid-quiz.

  • Continue card first
  • Settings in a sheet, not a bar
  • Feedback sheet, Continue fixed
  • Split neck: no scroll, 43×40 px cells

Text and accent

#f0f0f0 on bg 15.4:1 · #a0a0a0 on card 6.6:1 · #6ee7b7 on bg 11.4:1 · primary #34d399 with #0f0f0f text 9.8:1 · #f87171 on bg 6.3:1. All AA or better.

Targets and type

Buttons 52 px (44 px for icon and small buttons), answers 60 px, fretboard cells ≈ 43 × 40 px on a 390 px phone. Type: 16 body, 19 prompt, 22 titles, 46 score. Focus: 2 px #6ee7b7 outline, 3 px offset.

Notation

Light paper (#fafafa) with black ink, as VexFlow renders today; panels get a 1 px #e5e5e5 edge so they read as objects on the dark card. Correct/wrong always pair colour with an icon and words.
