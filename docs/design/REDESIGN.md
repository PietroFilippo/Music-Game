# UI redesign: audit, three directions, palette and spec

Mocks: `mocks/redesign-index.html` (overview, audit summary, palette comparison, type scale) links to one board per direction: `mocks/redesign-a.html` (Stage), `mocks/redesign-b.html` (Rail) and `mocks/redesign-c.html` (Desk). Every board shows Home, a lesson step, the three quiz states, results, Stats, the Tuner and Settings at 1920 × 1032 (a maximized desktop window), 1366 × 768, 1100 × 720 (the desktop app's default window) and 390 × 844, with one Portuguese check per direction. The boards are generated by `mocks/redesign.build.mjs` (`node docs/design/mocks/redesign.build.mjs`), which ports the geometry of `Fretboard.tsx` so the necks in the mocks are the real neck. Nothing in `src/` or `src-tauri/` changed.

## Decisions (approved 2026-10-16, implemented)

1. **Direction:** A, Stage, with every recommended answer in section 9: phone feedback in flow with a sticky Continue, Bahnschrift for readouts, green-tinted greys, filled buttons with 2.7–3 : 1 outlines, Learn as text on desktop and an icon on phones, two panes from 1000 px, Enter skips the automatic wait, sentence-case labels, a Lesson button on results.
2. **Notation (section 10):** N1 ink on slate for every staff, N2 linked tab for every tab, the asked note ringed in the rhythm quiz, the larger tab and staff pair, off-white piano keys.

Implementation notes that differ from or add to the spec:
- Notation scales through one CSS property, `--notation-scale`, which layouts set: about 2 in the lesson panel, 1.6 for the explorer's pair there, 1.1 to 1.4 in one-column lessons, and in the quiz pane whatever fits beside the solution neck (`StageNotation` in `src/components/Stage.tsx`). Each drawing keeps its VexFlow layout and only its SVG is scaled.
- The feedback pair is drawn at 156 px up to 1299 px wide (not 1199), because two 200 px drawings do not fit the quiz column below about 1300 px; 200 px from 1300 and 260 px from 1600.
- The tab numbers clear their box in the colour actually behind the drawing (read from the page), so no per-panel `--notation-bg` token is needed.
- Line and space numbers on teaching staves are smaller than the gap between lines, so neighbouring numbers no longer touch (they overlapped before the redesign).
- The rhythm lesson's measure has no lit string; only the rhythm quiz lights the string of the note it asks about, as in the board.
- Lesson steps carry an optional `visual` next to `body`, so the two-pane layout can put the copy on the left and the visual in the panel; a step counts as done once visited.
- In the 1100 × 720 window an answered round can be taller than the window; Continue stays pinned to the bottom of the column and the column scrolls.

## The brief

"Have a redesign of the UI. Use space more effectively, specially on fullscreen. Still be clean with the design. You can change the colors palette too if wanted, look for ui and ux improvements, etc. Keep in the theme too."

Read as: large windows first (1920 × 1080 and up must stop looking like a phone column), calm and uncluttered, same identity (dark, mint, paper for notation, the existing icons), and a look beyond layout for real UX fixes. Hard constraints: React and plain CSS, no new libraries; both languages (the longest topic title is 45 characters); keyboard operation, visible focus, 44 px targets, no meaning by colour alone, reduced motion; the desktop caption-button corner (138 × 32, top right) free on every screen; phones keep working, including the split neck.

## 2. Three directions

All three share the palette, type scale, tokens and the independent UX fixes in section 7. They differ in how they use width.

### A, Stage (recommended)

One page, no new navigation. Content fills the window up to a comfortable measure, and every quiz becomes a two-pane stage: the neck or the staff in the wide left pane, scaling with the window; the prompt, the answers and then the feedback in a fixed right column. Nothing covers the question and Continue never moves. Home becomes a course board: the Continue band across the top, then the four modules side by side with their topic lists, rings and Review buttons; a maximized 1920 window shows the whole course without scrolling. Lessons split the same way: step list and text on the left, a large visual panel on the right, Previous/Next under the text.

Trade-offs: two layouts for the quiz (two-pane from 1000 px, stacked below) that `GameShell` renders from one state; wide boards need a cap (1160 px) so SVG labels do not grow past 25 px; Home on 1366 × 768 still scrolls about one screen; no global navigation beyond the three icons.

### B, Rail

A persistent 248 px left rail with Continue, the four modules (progress ring and due badge), Stats, Tuner and Settings. The main area shows one module at a time as a wide list with per-topic bars. In lessons and quizzes the rail collapses to 64 px, the visual takes the centre at up to 1200 px, and feedback docks as a 420 px right-hand drawer that pushes the board rather than covering it. Settings is a drawer too. Phones get module chips under the wordmark and share A's single column.

Trade-offs: the most new code (rail, module routes, collapsed state, drawer, module page); less overview (one module at a time); the drawer squeezes the neck to about 540 px at 1100 × 720, exactly when the learner compares their tap with the answer; two chrome systems (rail and top bar) and more drag-region work for the desktop window.

### C, Desk

Paper above, instrument below. The upper area holds prompt, staff, tab, answers and explanations; a full-width instrument strip pinned to the bottom holds the neck, as wide as the window (1500 px at 1920). Lessons work the same way; the Tuner turns the strip into six string lanes that light up when heard and play when tapped. Home is a course sheet: all 25 topics in a table (two tables at 1600 px and up) plus a Today panel with Continue, due reviews and recent plays. Stats is the same table with more columns; Settings is a two-column page.

Trade-offs: 1500 px boards scale labels to about 32 px and markers to 60 px, so `Fretboard` would need a size-aware mode; half the course (Module 1, much of 3 and 4) has no neck, so the strip is often empty; at 1100 × 720 the strip takes a third of the height and long explanations scroll; phones fall back to A, so there are two mental models.

## 3. Recommendation

Build **A, Stage**, with two borrowings: C's rule that the neck is never capped below the pane (A caps at 1160 px, not 560), and B's "due" badge on module headers and the Stats tile.

Why A: it fixes the two complaints the owner can see at 1920 (the small neck in an empty screen, the sheet over the question) with the fewest new concepts. It changes `styles.css`, `Menu.tsx`, `GameShell.tsx`, `LessonShell.tsx`, `Stats.tsx`, `Tuner.tsx` and the props of `Fretboard` and the VexFlow wrappers, and nothing else structural. The desktop caption corner stays trivially free because the top bar still spans the window with its 148 px right padding. Phones keep their layout and gain the in-flow feedback. B is the right choice only if the owner wants modules to become places to go; C is the most striking and the least honest about a course where half the questions have no neck.

## 5. Type and spacing

Two families, two jobs, nothing downloaded. **Segoe UI Variable** (Text for body, Display for headings) is the Windows 11 system face and falls back to `system-ui` elsewhere; it has true optical sizes, so headings get tighter and body stays open. **Bahnschrift**, also shipped with Windows 10 and 11, is the readout face: the results percentage, round counter, ✓/✗ counts, timer, cents, Hz, scores in Stats, fret numbers in tables. It is the typographic cousin of a tuner display; on other platforms it falls back to the display face with tabular numerals.

```css
--font: 'Segoe UI Variable Text', 'Segoe UI', system-ui, sans-serif;
--font-display: 'Segoe UI Variable Display', 'Segoe UI', system-ui, sans-serif;
--font-readout: 'Bahnschrift', 'Segoe UI Variable Display', 'Segoe UI', system-ui, sans-serif;
```

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--t-1` | 13px | captions, labels, chips, table headers |
| `--t0` | clamp(15px, 14px + 0.2vw, 17px) | body, buttons, lesson copy (line-height 1.65, max 62ch) |
| `--t1` | clamp(16px, 15px + 0.25vw, 18px) | row titles, top-bar title, card titles |
| `--t2` | clamp(19px, 17px + 0.5vw, 24px) | question prompt, sheet titles |
| `--t3` | clamp(22px, 19px + 0.8vw, 30px) | page and lesson headings, the Home hero title |
| `--t4` | clamp(44px, 32px + 2.6vw, 76px) | results percentage, tuner note (readout face) |
| `--t-answer` | clamp(18px, 16px + 0.4vw, 22px) | answer buttons |

At 390 px the scale is 13 / 15 / 16 / 19 / 22 / 44; at 1920 it is 13 / 17 / 18 / 24 / 30 / 76. Line length: body and lesson copy ≤ 62ch; explanations in the quiz column ≤ 48ch.

Labels: sentence case, `--t-1`, `--muted`, no letter-spacing. The seven uppercase labels of R3 become this one style. The module number ("Module 2") is a label, not an eyebrow.

Spacing: `--s1` 4, `--s2` 8, `--s3` 12, `--s4` 16, `--s5` 24, `--s6` 32, `--s7` 48 px; `--gutter: clamp(16px, 2vw, 40px)`. Radii: `--r-card` 16 px (cards, panels, dialogs), `--r-ctl` 12 px (buttons, answers, inputs), 999 px pills. Targets: `--tap` 52 px, `--tap-sm` 44 px; nothing interactive below 44 × 44.

## 6. Layout grid and breakpoints

Breakpoints are on viewport width (container width on `#root` in the desktop build, which already has `container-type: inline-size`).

| Name | Width | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XS | < 480 | phone: single column, split interactive neck (frets 0–6 and 7–12), bottom sheet for Settings, in-flow feedback with a sticky Continue |
| S | 480–767 | single column, whole neck up to 560 px, answers 2 × 2 |
| M | 768–999 | single column with a wider measure (page max 880 px), neck up to 760 px, Settings as a centered dialog |
| L | 1000–1599 | two-pane quiz and lesson; Home, Stats, Results and Tuner in two columns; neck up to the pane width |
| XL | ≥ 1600 | Home and Stats in four columns; neck capped at 1160 px; staff capped at 720 px |

The desktop window (default 1100 × 720, minimum 400 × 560) therefore opens in L and can be resized down to XS.

Page: no fixed `max-width` on `.page`; `padding: 0 var(--gutter)`; content blocks have their own caps (quiz column 32 % ≥ 360 px; Results 1400 px; Tuner 1200 px; Settings dialog 560/620 px). Column gap `--s5` (24 px) everywhere; card padding `--s4`.

Top bar: 56 px, icons 44 × 44 at y = 8, title `--t1`; the desktop build keeps `padding-right: 148px` (the page is full width, so the existing collision formula resolves to 148 px on every screen). Drag regions as in `DESKTOP.md`.

## 7. Spec of Direction A, per screen and breakpoint

### 7.1 Home (`Menu.tsx`)

- **Hero band** (L, XL): one row, `--surface` with the existing green gradient from the left, `--r-card`, padding 18/22. Left: label "Continue" or "Up next", h2 `--t3` topic title, caption line "Module 2 · 10 rounds │ Best 70%, last 30%" (the separator is a 1 × 12 px rule, `--line-strong`, not a middle dot). Right: Practice (primary, play icon) and Lesson (secondary, book icon), `--tap` high, not stretched. Below L: the current hero card.
- **Module grid**: `grid-template-columns: repeat(4, minmax(0, 1fr))` at XL, 2 at L, 1 below; gap `--s4`; `align-items: start`.
- **Module card**: label "Module n"; h3 `--t1`; a 44 px ring (4 px stroke, `--line-strong` track, `--accent` fill) with "2/10" in the readout face; a 4 px bar under the header; then one of: Review weak spots · n (secondary `sm`, retry icon) when due > 0, "Nothing to review today" (`--muted`) when the module has history and nothing due, or the module description when it has no history yet.
- **Topic row**: min-height 56 px, `border-top: 1px solid var(--line)`; name `--t0` 600, clamped to two lines (the 45-character Portuguese title wraps once at every width ≥ 390); score line `--t-1` muted ("Best 90%, last 80%" or "Not played yet"); Learn as a ghost `sm` button with the book icon and its text at L and XL, icon-only 44 × 44 with `aria-label` below L; Practice primary `sm` (44 px). The current `article[aria-label]` stays so the tests keep working.
- **Collapsing**: at L and XL all modules are open (the grid absorbs the length). Below L the existing rule applies (modules with unplayed topics open, others collapsed) with "Show n topics" as the secondary `sm` button.
- **Keyboard**: unchanged; the Learn/Practice tab order is row by row.

### 7.2 Lesson (`LessonShell.tsx`)

- **L, XL**: `grid-template-columns: minmax(320px, 36%) minmax(0, 1fr)`, gap `--s6`, padding `4px var(--gutter) var(--s4)`. Left column: a step list (`ol`, one item per step: a 22 px number circle, done steps get a filled accent circle with ✓, the current step gets an `--elevated` row and an accent ring; items are buttons, 44 px high, `aria-current="step"` on the current one), then h2 `--t3`, then the copy (`--fg-2`, `--t0`, line-height 1.65, max 62ch), then Previous (secondary) and Next (primary, flex 1) at the bottom of the column (`margin-top: auto`). Right column: a visual panel (`--surface`, `--line` border, `--r-card`, padding `--s5`) with the visual centered and Listen buttons under it. The fretboard is `width: 100%; max-width: 980px`; the explorer, measures and staff diagrams get the same treatment (section 7.9).
- **Below L**: today's layout with the new tokens: segment strip, "Step 3 of 6" label, centered heading and copy, visual, sticky Previous/Next.
- Step titles come from `LessonStep.title`; a step without one shows "Step n".
- **Keyboard**: ← and → change steps when focus is on the body; the Next button keeps focus after a click so Enter repeats.

### 7.3 Quiz, question state (`GameShell.tsx`, `FretboardQuiz.tsx`, `ModuleOneGame.tsx`, `AnswerBank.tsx`)

- **Progress row** (all widths): under the top bar; segments (max 420 px wide), "Round n of 10" (600), ✓ and ✗ pills in the readout face, timer ring (40 px) when timed. Still a drag region on desktop.
- **L, XL**: `.stage { display: grid; grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr) minmax(360px, 32%); gap: var(--s5); padding: 0 var(--gutter) var(--s4); flex: 1; min-height: 0 }`. Left `.vis`: flex, centered both ways. Right `.col`: flex column, gap `--s4`, a spacer `.grow` before the bottom elements.
  - Fretboard questions: the board at `width: 100%` (max 1160 px at XL) with `interactive` geometry; the target string (when the question names one) gets the lane plus the thick accent string; the keyboard focus ring (existing) is visible on the current cell. Column: prompt (`--t2`, 600, left-aligned), hint `--muted` ("Tap the position on the neck. Arrow keys move, Enter selects."), spacer.
  - Choice questions with a staff: the staff rendered at the pane's width × 0.7, capped at 720 px (VexFlow `Staff width/height` props driven by a `useElementWidth` hook on `.vis`); column: prompt, then `AnswerBank` in one column (`answers.col`): each answer `min-height: 60px`, text left, a `kbd` "1"–"4" at the right (shown only under `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`), hover border `--accent`; spacer; hint "Press 1–4 to answer."
  - Choice questions with a board (intervals, octaves, scales): the board left, answers right; same as above.
  - Text visuals (interval spelling, sound properties): the text visual left at `--t3`/`--t4`, answers right.
- **M and below**: today's stacked layout: prompt centered, visual, `AnswerBank` 2 × 2 (one column for long labels), the split neck under 480 px.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: `1`–`9` pick the nth visible answer; `Enter` or `Space` presses Continue when a round is answered (manual mode) or skips the wait (automatic mode); shortcuts are ignored while the Settings sheet is open or focus is in an input. Hints appear only on hover-capable pointers.

### 7.4 Quiz, feedback state

- **L, XL**: no fixed sheet. The column replaces its question-state content with: verdict (Correct! / Let's review / Time is up, with icon), "Answer: …" when wrong or timed out, the explanation (`--fg-2`, ≤ 48ch), Listen (when the question has a sound), the tab + staff pair (220 px each at XL, 180 at L ≥ 1200, 156 at 1000–1199), the spacer, and Continue (`btn-block`, primary) with a right-aligned "Enter ↵" hint, or the auto-advance fill plus "auto · 0.9 s" in automatic mode. For choice questions the answers stay visible above the verdict with their ok/bad/dim states.

### The change requested

Direction A is approved with the recommended answers to section 9. The one change: the white notation cards (the Tab and Staff cards in quiz feedback, every `StaffDiagram` in the lessons) glare on the dark UI and look pasted in. Notation should belong to the same visual language as the fretboard drawing, and no lesson may get harder to read; the goal is that reading notation gets clearer.

### Directions

| | Surface | Lines | Glyphs | Highlight | Tab |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Today | #fafafa card, #e5e5e5 border | black | black | #059669 | TAB sign, black numbers |

**Recommendation: N1 as the base for all five components, N2 for every tab** (`GuitarTab`, `PositionNotation`, the tab half of `MeasureNotation`). N3 is a fallback if paper must stay a brand trait; it removes the glare (12.7 : 1 against the page instead of 16.7 : 1) but keeps a pasted-in rectangle and a second green.

Why it reads better, not only looks better:

- One colour means "this note" everywhere: the staff note, its label, the neck marker and the lit tab string are all the mint. Today the staff highlight is a different green on white.
- Nothing competes with the glyphs: lines are a step below the strings in contrast, glyphs are the foreground colour, numbers and names are the label colour. Hierarchy is lines < labels < glyphs < highlight, in that order.
- Lessons that stack a staff over a neck (Treble Clef step 5, Clefs step 5, Staff I step 5, Module 2 "One note, several places") stop flipping the eye between a bright card and a dark board.
- The linked tab is visibly a picture of the six strings in the board's orientation (string 1 on top), which is the commonest tab confusion in the Strings and Tuning lesson. The lit line answers "which string" before the number answers "which fret".
- In the rhythm measure the lit string shows the whole phrase sits on one string; the proposed halo on the asked note makes "tab note 3" unambiguous in the duration quiz.
- Sizes are unchanged at every width (line spacing 10 px at 1×), so nothing gets smaller.

### Tokens

```css
--staff-line: #7c867e;       /* stave and tab lines, barlines: 4.3 : 1 on surface, 4.7 : 1 on page */
--notation-bg: var(--bg);    /* what tab numbers clear behind themselves; panels set it to var(--surface) */
--accent-soft: rgba(127,224,180,.18);  /* halo fill (already in the A palette) */
/* glyphs: var(--fg); highlight: var(--accent); numbers and string names: var(--muted); labels: var(--fg-2) */
/* --paper and --ink stay only for the Keyboard (see below) or for N3 */
```

Contrast (computed in `notation.build.mjs`): lines 4.3 / 4.7 : 1 (target 3), glyphs and ledger lines 14.7 / 16.2 : 1, highlight 10.3 / 11.3 : 1, numbers and string names 6.3 / 6.9 : 1 (target 4.5), labels 10.2 : 1, highlighted label 10.3 : 1. Highlight against the glyph colour is 1.4 : 1, which is why the highlight never relies on colour alone: a 1.5 px ring at radius 10.5 with the 18 % fill, a bold label, and in the tab a 3 px string under the number.

Minimum sizes at 390 px, measured in the prototype: line spacing 10 px; whole-note head about 11 × 7 px; ledger lines 1 px in the glyph colour; tab numbers 11 pt (≈ 15 px) in the readout face, bold; string names 11 px bold; line and space numbers 12 px; note labels 13 px; captions 13 px sentence case.

### Per-component spec

Common (a small `notationTheme.ts` helper, used by all five):

- After `renderer.getContext()`: `ctx.setBackgroundFillStyle('var(--notation-bg)')` (VexFlow's `TabNote` draws a `clearRect` behind each number with this colour; today it is white), `ctx.setFillStyle('var(--fg)')`, `ctx.setStrokeStyle('var(--fg)')`. SVG presentation attributes accept `var()`, so the drawing follows the theme without re-rendering.
- `stave.setStyle({ strokeStyle: 'var(--staff-line)', fillStyle: 'var(--staff-line)' })`; `stave.setDefaultLedgerLineStyle` in `--fg`; for each `stave.getModifiers()`: Barline category in `--staff-line`, everything else (clef, time signature) in `--fg`.
- Notes: `note.setStyle` and `note.setLedgerLineStyle` in `--fg`, or `--accent` when highlighted. Beams: `beam.setStyle` in `--fg`.
- Halo: format first (`Formatter.formatToStave`, `voice.setStave`, `note.setStave`), then `ctx.arc` at `(getNoteHeadBeginX + getNoteHeadEndX) / 2, getYs()[0]`, r 10.5, fill `--accent-soft`, stroke `--accent` 1.5 px, then `voice.draw`. (`FormatAndDraw` is replaced by these three calls so the halo can sit behind the head.)
- Text: numbers and string names `ctx.setFont(readout face, 11–12, 600)`; labels `ctx.setFont(text face, 13, 500 | 700)`, centred with `ctx.measureText`.
- The width prop from the container (`useElementWidth`, section 7.9) is applied as a `viewBox` + CSS width on the finished SVG, so VexFlow's layout is untouched; caps 720 (staff in a quiz), 748 (lesson diagrams, 2.2×), 850 (measure, 1.7×).
